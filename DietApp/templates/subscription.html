Frequently asked questions

What happens after I tap Pay?

{% if payment_ready %}A payment request is sent to your selected Mobile Money number. Check your phone and follow the approval instructions there.{% else %}The Pay button currently explains that payments are not connected, and no request is sent. Once Mobile Money is enabled, a request will arrive on your phone for approval.{% endif %}

Where do I enter my Mobile Money PIN?

Enter your PIN only on your phone in the official Airtel Money or MTN authorization prompt. Never enter it on the Diet Services website or share it with anyone.

When does my subscription become active?

Your subscription becomes active after the payment is confirmed. If it is pending, check your phone for the approval request.

What if I don’t receive a prompt?

Check that the number belongs to the selected network and is reachable. Avoid retrying while a request is pending; contact support if no prompt arrives.

Does Diet Services store my PIN?

No. Your PIN is entered with the mobile operator’s approval flow and is never requested or stored by Diet Services.
